THINGS TO DECIDE, NOT THINGS I DECIDED
- مهگل (Mahgol) and نیلا (Nila) appear to share the exact same ceramic base
  (same paisley cloth, same table, same dome shape) — Nila's only visible
  difference is a two-tone navy-patterned shade. They may be the same base
  sold with a shade option, not two separate products. I kept them as two
  catalog entries since that's how they were named, but this is worth
  confirming before pricing them separately.
- اقاقیا (Aghaghia) has a tapered CONE base; مهگل (Mahgol) has a rounded
  DOME base. They use a similar oval-dot texture but are genuinely
  different shapes — not the same mislabeling risk as Aghaghia vs its own
  tall version, which really do look alike.
- پیرکس (Pirex)'s material was "poly" (polyester) in the original data —
  a guess inherited from the collection-level brief, never checked per
  item. A photo named «جاشمعی پیرکس» (Pyrex candle holder) arrived showing
  glass taper candlesticks — Pyrex is a glass brand, so I've corrected
  pirex and pirex-u to material "glass". The photo is installed as
  pirex.jpg but still badged, because a pair of candlesticks isn't a
  confirmed match for "three heights, polyester" as originally described —
  the shape fits, the material now fits, but nobody's said yes.
- رز (Roz) — a lamp with a rose-embroidered shade — is a different product
  from رزه (Roze), the rose-shaped candle already in the catalog. Similar
  names, unrelated items; don't merge them.
